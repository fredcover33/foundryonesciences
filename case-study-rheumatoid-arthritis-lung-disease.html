Case study · Rheumatology · Pulmonology

Rheumatoid arthritis, and now the lungs are failing.

Illustrative case · built from a test case · no real patient
Rheumatoid arthritis, seropositive, 11 years, on methotrexate and adalimumab, now with 4 months of progressive dyspnea, dry cough and new bilateral lower-lobe reticulation on CT, with quiescent joints.
What the research surfaced

Read across the specialties, in one place.

  • The cause the evidence stands behind: RA-associated interstitial lung disease. High-titer RF and anti-CCP, 11 years of seropositive disease and a 30-pack-year history give this patient the dominant risk profile. Two large independent studies show seropositive RA carries a ninefold hazard for ILD, with the NSIP subtype tied to seropositivity.
  • Two drug suspects, neither cleared. Amiodarone (13 months) and nitrofurantoin (6 months) both have the means and the opportunity. Amiodarone is the better documented: it was the most common drug causing ILD in one referral-center series, and the rising TSH, hepatic hyperattenuation and corneal deposits in the record fit systemic accumulation. Nitrofurantoin can cause both acute eosinophilic and subacute fibrosing disease. TMP-SMX (4 months) is a suspect on thinner evidence.
  • Methotrexate has a partial alibi. A case-control study with international replication found it inversely associated with chronic fibrosing RA-ILD (pooled adjusted odds ratio 0.43). It is not cleared.
  • The pivotal open question is the BAL cell differential. Dominant eosinophilia would favor drug hypersensitivity; lymphocytosis with a low CD4/CD8 ratio would favor methotrexate pneumonitis or RA-ILD; a mixed neutrophilic-lymphocytic picture would favor fibrotic RA-ILD. The research also notes that a normal BNP does not exclude pulmonary hypertension.
The next steps the research supports

What would settle it.

  • BAL cell differential with eosinophil and lymphocyte percentages and the CD4/CD8 ratio.
  • Myositis antibody panel, to exclude antisynthetase syndrome as another upstream cause.
  • Pulmonary hypertension evaluation: echocardiography, and consideration of right heart catheterization.
