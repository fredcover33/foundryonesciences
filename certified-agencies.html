What Companion does

The doctor's signed work,
straight to your office.

Some patients have three or four things going on at once. The MRS Engine with Companion is built for them. The doctor enters the case, and the engine reads the published research across the specialties and writes a Chart Note.

A Chart Note is a two-page summary of the case: what the research found, the sources behind it, and the billing signals. The doctor marks every signal himself and signs it. Then it goes straight to his billing agency, with a case number and never a patient's name.

The doctor decides what goes on the Chart Note. The agency decides what to bill. Companion carries his signed work to your office, so you can see what he did and where it sits in his record.
