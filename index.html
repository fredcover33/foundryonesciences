A distributed medical think engine

The MRS Engine uses card game mechanics as a structural metaphor for how biological research is classified, compared, and cross-referenced. Each of the four disease domains corresponds to a suit — enabling systematic mining of cross-domain intersections that no single-discipline approach can replicate.

Outputs are classified through the JELLO insight framework, scored through the Reasonable Man Standard, and captured as drug hypotheses, formulation protocols, and clinical intelligence assets.

01
JELLO Classification Insight moments scored by biological depth (Red → Violet) and cross-domain source discipline
02
AAD Scanner v2.0 MeSH, SNOMED-CT, UMLS, Gene Ontology taxonomy layer with ETL-style pipeline staging
03
Reasonable Man Standard Human-judgment quality filter ensuring every hypothesis meets a defensible evidentiary threshold
04
Version-Controlled Discovery Logs Every hypothesis is timestamped, versioned, and preserved as intellectual property evidence

The Founder

Frederick W. Cover II

Foundry One Sciences was founded by Frederick W. Cover II — a U.S. Navy veteran, 24-year chemical operations professional, and the sole inventor across the company's patent portfolio.

Fred conceived the Medical Research Solitaire framework as a way to impose structure on the chaos of cross-domain medical literature — bringing the systematic discipline of manufacturing operations to the fluid, high-stakes world of drug hypothesis development.

With a corporate law background from Widener University and deep operational experience, Fred built Foundry One Sciences as a company where every hypothesis is defensible, every patent is filed with precision, and the science never stops.
